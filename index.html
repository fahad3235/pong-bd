How to play

  • Move your left paddle with the mouse, touch drag, W/S or arrow keys.
  • The ball speeds up after every paddle hit. Angle depends on where it strikes the paddle.
  • Easy CPU is slow and sloppy, Medium tracks well, Hard predicts bounces.
  • First to 5, 7 or 11 points wins the match.
